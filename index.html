Chapter 06 • Multi-Device Sync & Privacy

Real-Time Multi-Device AutoSync & Private Google Drive Backup

Use Monetic seamlessly across multiple phones and tablets. Real-time Firebase AutoSync propagates your transactions instantaneously, while Google Drive provides private 1:1 backups — completely free for all users.

  • 1

    Firebase Real-Time AutoSync Across Multiple Devices

    Log a transaction on your smartphone and watch it appear instantaneously on your tablet or secondary device in real time. Firebase listeners handle real-time state synchronization seamlessly in the background without manual imports.

  • 2

    Private 1:1 Google Drive Backups

    Backups are saved directly into your personal Google Drive account in the hidden application data folder (appDataFolder). 100% isolated, confidential, and costs $0 in cloud storage fees.

  • 3

    Offline-First Resilience

    No internet connection needed to log transactions or track budgets. Everything is stored locally in SQLite Room. The moment your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or cellular data, AutoSync syncs everything effortlessly.

  • 4

    Hardware-Backed PIN, Biometrics & Right to Erasure

    PINs are hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100,000 rounds) and protected in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain. Biometric credentials never leave your device's hardware Secure Enclave, with 1-tap permanent data erasure anytime.
